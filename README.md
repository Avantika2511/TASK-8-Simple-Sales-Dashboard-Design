## 📝 Insights (based on data):

1. The **Technology** category had the **highest sales**, especially due to items like **Laptops and Phones**.
2. The **West** region had strong performance, particularly in **Furniture** and **Office Supplies**.
3. **August and October** were peak months for sales, likely driven by **Laptops** and **Office supplies** demand.
4. Products with **0% discount** tended to yield the **highest profits**.
